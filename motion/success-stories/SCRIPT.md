# Success stories, SG MOTION 9:16, 30 s maximum (script draft v1)

**Style:** Egyptian Arabic, like our scripts: a question to the audience, «طيب…», short lines, an excited CTA.
**VO:** Saber's cloned voice. **Fps:** 25. **Music:** upbeat, starting from the track's start. **SFX:** one UI sound per motion.

Rules before production:
- Mariam must approve showing her name and her work.
- Chats are redrawn as kit bubbles: no screenshots, no numbers, no profile pictures.
- Every Rasasi design carries the label «مشروع تدريبي» (Rasasi is not our client).
- No promises of jobs or income.
