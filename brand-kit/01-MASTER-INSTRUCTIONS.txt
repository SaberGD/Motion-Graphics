LOCK THE ASSETS

Do not typeset, redraw, trace, regenerate, distort, recolor, crop, invert, bevel or morph this logo. Animate only the layer container: opacity, position, scale or a simple reveal mask. Keep the final full logo visible and intact.
2. The white lettering and orange AI badge are part of the supplied artwork. Do not turn the whole logo black in a light scene. Prefer a charcoal logo plate on a light frame. If a different approved variant is required but absent, request it.

If doing image-to-video, use these as identity anchors, keep camera motion restrained, and reject any identity drift. Expression sheets are reference-only; not automatically animation-ready assets.

VISUAL LANGUAGE
Charcoal, warm red, carefully limited orange, off-white type, quiet construction grids, technical alignment and restrained clipped corners. Spacious composition; one idea and one primary focal point per scene. The assets should look intentional and professional, never crowded, cheap, overly futuristic or aggressively salesy.

Use the chosen motion palette throughout one film:
Background #0A0A0A; surfaces #151515 / #1C1C1C; primary red #C4321F; deep red #7A1B12; orange #FF8A1F; type #F6F3F2; secondary type #C7C9CD. Existing logo and mascot pixels are exempt from flat fill matching. Do not recolor originals to force a palette ratio.
Aim for approximately 75-85% neutral visual area, 10-15% warm red and at most 5% orange emphasis. This is a direction, not a mathematical constraint on photographs. Never fill the entire film with bright orange or dark navy. Green is for genuine semantic success only, not a second brand accent.

Do not randomly swap in the observed per-system red variants or utility themes. In particular, accounting utility themes, semantic badge colors, selected UI colors and old repository defaults are not an alternative marketing identity.

At 1080px reference width, use a 72px grid with 1px off-white lines at approximately 2.5% opacity. Scale geometric units with output width. Use the supplied grid/corner SVGs or reproduce their geometry exactly. Limit corner cuts to two anchor areas; use 17px clipped corners and approximately 7px UI corner radius when needed. Panels are for actual content or tools, not nested decorative cards.
Atmosphere can use a very faint burgundy edge wash. Avoid floating gradient blobs, bokeh, decorative orbs, busy sci-fi HUDs, scanline noise, crypto graphics and heavy glow. Existing light emitted by the mascot is acceptable; do not add glowing clouds around it.

TYPOGRAPHY / ARABIC
Use Cairo for Arabic. Default headlines 700, body 500, slant 0, letter-spacing 0. Use Bebas Neue for short English display labels. Tajawal is an approved fallback when Cairo cannot be used, not a third family to mix arbitrarily. Do not rebuild any logo using these fonts.
Arabic must be correctly shaped and RTL. Keep English product names, numbers and URLs in isolated LTR runs. Do not split Arabic into disconnected animated letters. Animate whole words or lines. Do not add tracking, fake kashida, artificial italic, stretching or random diacritics.
Keep titles modest. In a 1080x1920 film, start around 64px for Arabic headlines and 36px for supporting text. Use 3-7 words in a headline, at most two lines, and at most one concise supporting sentence. On 1920x1080, start around 72px / 36px. These are production defaults, not extracted UI font sizes. Reflow or shorten long copy before shrinking it. Keep subtitles legible on a phone. No text should obscure the subject, the mascot's face or chest, or another line of text.
The supplied style-frame PNGs contain examples only. Do not reuse their campaign copy as factual claims without a brief.

MOTION GRAMMAR
Use cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1), matching the observed Maro easing. Proposed production frame rate: 25fps. Use a 12-frame entrance (480ms), 8-frame exit (320ms), 2-frame stagger (80ms), and 20-frame logo reveal (800ms). Quantize cuts to whole frames. Other durations must be explicitly approved for the placement.
Movement should communicate hierarchy: one anchor settles, then the supporting element appears. Use 12-24px movement at a 1080px reference width, restrained opacity or masked reveals, and straight cuts. Do not default to springs, repeated bounce, elastic squash, glitch, spinning logos or repeated zoom transitions. Do not apply camera motion and object motion to everything at once.
An intact mascot may drift by at most about 6px in an idle loop. Avoid free camera orbit or fake parallax that reveals invented geometry. If a slow camera move is necessary, stay within about 1.02x scale. An 8-second supplied preview is a silent composition-motion reference, not a final campaign or proof of a 3D rig.
Hold the end card fully readable for at least 2 seconds; prefer 3 seconds. No strobe, rapid alternating high-contrast flashes or motion that makes reading difficult. For interactive previews, respect reduced-motion preferences and provide pause/replay.

FRAMING / OUTPUT
Use one of: portrait 1080x1920, landscape 1920x1080, or square 1080x1080. Recompose for each ratio; never simply crop a finished layout.
Portrait conservative safe content box: x=86..994, y=192..1574. Landscape: x=115..1805, y=86..994. Square: x=86..994, y=86..994. These are suggested production guards, not guaranteed platform overlay specifications. Check the actual target placement before publication. Keep logos, primary copy, CTA and faces inside the safe content box. Decorative texture may bleed.

If the tool supports editable layers, import exact logo and font assets, create actual text layers, and lock identity layers.

Do not promise perfect brand consistency from a prompt alone. If the tool cannot attach references or respect locked assets, say so and propose the textless-plate workflow.
Do not repeatedly regenerate the whole mascot just to move it; reuse the provided raster pose. Reuse approved plates and motion presets for continuity between shots.
