# Pre-flight / pass-fail

Reject the export when any identity, data or factual check fails. Small aesthetic preferences may be revised after these gates pass.

- [ ] Correct brand mode: Academy or Maro; no accidental HORIX identity.
- [ ] The supplied original logo is placed intact, with original colors and source aspect ratio.
- [ ] Logo source/export matches the asset manifest; no redraw, hallucinated SVG or black inversion.
- [ ] Clear space and composition guards are respected; important content is not under target-platform controls.
- [ ] Arabic is connected, proofread, RTL and legible on an actual phone.
- [ ] English product names, numbers and URLs have correct LTR isolation.
- [ ] Headline remains modest; at most two lines, no giant words or accidental text overlap.
- [ ] Cairo and the chosen Latin display font are loaded; no silent font substitution.
- [ ] Brand palette is consistent; operational theme colors did not leak into the marketing palette.
- [ ] Grid and corners stay subordinate; no noisy HUD, decorative blobs or excessive glow.
- [ ] One main movement/focal point at a time; no jitter, unnecessary bounce, strobe or rapid glitch.
- [ ] Mascot matches the original in every frame; no mirrored chest text, extra limbs or altered face.
- [ ] Flat PNG animation is not represented as a supplied 3D rig or novel pose asset.
- [ ] No real student/client data, passwords, financial figures, chats or private screens are visible.
- [ ] Price, offer, course name, dates, phone and CTA are approved for this specific campaign.
- [ ] No promised earnings, employment, certificates or product functions without current approved evidence.
- [ ] All music, voice, photos and illustrations have a supplied/verified right to use.
- [ ] Output dimensions and fps match the brief; each cut is on an integer frame.
- [ ] End card has at least 2 seconds of fully readable hold, ideally 3 seconds.
- [ ] SDR colors, audio level, clipping, compression and platform preview have been reviewed.
- [ ] The delivered file actually exists and plays; project/editability/alpha claims match real supported formats.
- [ ] Textless plate, exact copy and asset/rights notes are included when required.

Review at normal playback and pause on transitions, mascot gestures, logo reveal and end card. Automated checks support, but cannot replace, human Arabic and brand review.
